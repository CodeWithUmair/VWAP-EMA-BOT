# CRT + TBS: The Professional Guide to Liquidity Purge Strategies

## 1. Strategic Overview: The Synergy of CRT and Turtle Body Soup

The integration of Candle Range Theory (CRT) with Turtle Body Soup (TBS) represents a high-alpha evolution in technical architecture, transitioning beyond retail price action into the mechanics of institutional liquidity cycles. This synthesis allows the strategist to move from passive observation to the active exploitation of liquidity purges that facilitate institutional positioning. By aligning Higher Time Frame (HTF) structural intent with Lower Time Frame (LTF) execution precision, this framework isolates high-probability reversal points with clinical accuracy. The core mission of this manual is to codify a systematic, rules-based protocol for identifying and executing **Model 1** setups within the CRT environment.

## 2. Core Fundamentals: Defining the "Turtle Soup" Phenomenon

In institutional environments, liquidity manipulation at established structural boundaries is the primary engine of market reversals. The "Turtle Soup" model operates on the axiom that significant liquidity clusters (resting orders) reside at previous highs and lows. When price is driven to engage these clusters, a "purge" occurs, typically serving as the precursor to a directional shift as smart money absorbs the liquidity.

### Operational Definitions

- **Turtle Soup:** The mechanical process of price manipulating or "purging" previous highs or lows to capture resting liquidity.
- **Bearish Turtle Soup:** A specific architecture where price purges liquidity above an established high, followed by a confirmed bearish distribution cycle.
- **Bullish Turtle Soup:** A sequence where price purges liquidity below an established low, initiating a bullish distribution cycle.

While the conceptual foundation is simple, professional execution is contingent upon the granular analysis of candle anatomy at the point of manipulation.

## 3. Variations of Manipulation: TBS vs. TWWS

The structural integrity of a setup is determined by the nature of the candle close relative to the identified liquidity level. Identifying whether price exhibits sustained acceptance beyond a level or immediate rejection is the primary filter for trade quality.

### TBS vs. TWWS: Structural Comparison

| Feature | Turtle Body Soup (TBS) | Turtle Beak Soup (TWWS) |
|---|---|---|
| **Price Interaction** | Candle closes with its full body beyond the identified level | Candle "sweeps" the level with a wick (the "beak") |
| **Institutional Signal** | Sustained price acceptance; high-conviction purge | Immediate price rejection; lower-conviction sweep |
| **Probability Rating** | High: superior consistency and distribution potential | Moderate/Low: prone to premature failure |
| **Actionability** | Standard execution for full distribution targets | Requires aggressive management at friction points |

### Architectural Cues

Strategists must differentiate between "Sustained Acceptance" and "Wick Rejection." In a Bullish TBS, the manipulating candle breaches the low and maintains that breach via a full body close, signaling institutional intent to value the new range. In a Turtle Beak Soup (TWWS), the price sweeps the level but fails to sustain the breach, retreating back into the previous range. This distinction is the primary determinant of the trade's probability of reaching its distribution target.

## 4. The CRT Framework: Higher Time Frame (HTF) Analysis

To mitigate the risk of low-fidelity signals, all structural foundations must be established on the Higher Time Frame (HTF). This eliminates market noise and ensures that execution is grounded in institutional intent.

### HTF Rules & Parameters

- **HTF Selection:** Structural analysis is restricted to the 4-Hour (4H) or 1-Hour (1H) charts.
- **CRT Candle & Key Level Identification:** The strategist must mark "Candle 1" (the CRT candle) and identify a clean key level. This defines the operational environment.
- **The "Anti-Mixing" Rule:** Structural integrity is contingent upon the strict separation of analysis and execution. TBS and TWWS logic must not be applied during HTF analysis. These tools are reserved exclusively for the Lower Time Frame execution theater.

Once the HTF structural foundation is set, the transition to the execution phase begins.

## 5. Multi-Timeframe Execution: The Lower Time Frame (LTF) Workflow

The 5-Minute (5M) chart is the designated execution theater for Model 1 entries. This is where the HTF intent is validated through a specific manipulation and distribution sequence.

### Step-by-Step Execution Model

1. **Confirmation Switch:** Following the identification of the 4H/1H CRT candle and key level, immediately transition to the 5M chart.
2. **The Manipulation Sequence (Model 1):** Execute the "Candle 1, 2, 3" logic on the 5M timeframe.
   - **Candle 1:** The established CRT candle from the HTF context.
   - **Candle 2:** The manipulation candle. This candle must purge the liquidity of Candle 1.
   - **Candle 3:** The distribution candle.
3. **Entry Trigger:** The entry is confirmed upon the close of Candle 2. A TBS (Body Close) provides the highest-conviction trigger for a Model 1 entry.

## 6. Trade Management: Performance Outcomes and Failure Points

Quantitative probability dictates that not all entries should be managed identically. The distinction between TBS and TWWS directly informs the trade management strategy and risk mitigation profile.

### Institutional Trade Management

- **TBS Superiority:** Turtle Body Soup setups demonstrate higher consistency. A strong body close confirms that liquidity has been effectively engaged, allowing for the targeting of full distribution levels.
- **TWWS Friction Points:** Turtle Beak Soup (TWWS) is a lower-probability configuration. The 50% level of the CRT candle (Candle 1) acts as a critical friction point. Management protocol requires trimming positions or moving stops to breakeven at this level, as TWWS setups frequently reverse before reaching 50% of the CRT range.
- **Distribution vs. Invalidation:** In a high-probability setup, Candle 3 initiates distribution without revisiting the manipulation point. A critical invalidation rule occurs if Candle 3 purges the extreme of Candle 2. If Candle 3 breaches the Candle 2 high (in a bearish setup) or low (in a bullish setup), the distribution has failed and the trade must be terminated.

## 7. Actionable Trading Rules: The Master Checklist

The strategic edge of this system is derived from rigid adherence to the ruleset. Institutional-grade precision requires the separation of analysis from tactical execution.

### Non-Negotiable Execution Rules

1. The Higher Time Frame (4H/1H) is used exclusively for CRT candle (Candle 1) and Key Level identification.
2. The Lower Time Frame (5M) is the mandatory environment for TBS/TWWS execution and Model 1 entry confirmation.
3. Prioritize TBS (Body Close) setups. Treat TWWS (Wick Sweep) as secondary, lower-probability opportunities.
4. Target the 50% CRT candle level as the primary friction point and partial take-profit level for all TWWS setups.
5. If Candle 3 purges the extreme of Candle 2, the structural setup is invalidated.

Adhering to these liquidity-based trading principles ensures that every market entry is backed by structural logic and clear institutional footprints. Disciplined setup selection and aggressive management of lower-probability formations are the hallmarks of a professional trading architect.
